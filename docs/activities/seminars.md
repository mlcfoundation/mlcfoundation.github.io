## XXV Bhopal Seminar 2027

**Contemporary Issues in Population and Development in India** <br>
20-22 January 2027 <br>

MLC Foundation and ‘Shyam’ Institute is organising the XXV Bhopal Seminar 2027: Contemporary Issues in Population and Development in India from 20 through 22 January 2027. Presentations, discussions and deliberations at the XXV Bhopal Seminar 2027 will be organised around the following sub-themes:

* Population growth, projection and ageing
* Fertility and mortality
* Migration and urbanisation
* Economic security, development inequality
* Social inclusion, Social protection
* Environmental protection, Climate change

### Participation

Original research papers are invited for presentation at the XXV Bhopal Seminar 2027 on any of the sub-themes and on any other issue related to demography and development in India. Research papers to be presented at the XXV Bhopal Seminar 2027 should be based on the empirical evidence derived from household surveys or registration system or service statistics and not on anecdotal or analogical experiences.
